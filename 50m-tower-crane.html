Executive Summary & Design Standards

The objective of this project was to design a high-capacity 50-meter tower crane inspired by the Kellogg crane, matching its footprint while delivering superior structural performance, thermal resilience, and stability under extreme wind conditions.

Structural proof of strength and safety compliance was governed by ASME B30.3-2019 Section 3-1.1, EN 14439:2006+A2 Section 5.2, and DIN 15018-1. Deflection criteria were established per CISC Design Guide 7 (Industrial Building Design, Section 2.2), restricting total vertical tip deflection to less than 0.3867 m.

FEA Results & Structural Safety Validation

The structural model was partitioned into two subsystems: the Top Section (Jib & Counter-Jib Boom) and the Bottom Section (40m Lattice Column). ANSYS FEA was used to evaluate four critical load cases: Full Hook Load, 100 mph Wind Load (12.2 kPa), Winter Thermal Contraction (-27°F), and Summer Thermal Expansion (105°F).

Euler Buckling & Parallel Axis Theorem Proof

To guarantee absolute column stability under combined dead weight, jib moment, and 25-tonne payload, an Euler buckling analysis was conducted on the 40m 4-column tower system assuming fixed-free boundary conditions.

Parallel Axis Theorem for Column Group Moment of Inertia:
Itotal = 4 × (Iself + A × d2)

Euler Critical Buckling Load:
Pcr = (π2 · E · Itotal) / (K · L)2

Where K = 2.0 (Fixed-Free condition), L = 40 m, E = 210 GPa (St 52-3 Steel).
Result: Pcr >> Papplied, confirming 0% risk of elastic buckling failure.
