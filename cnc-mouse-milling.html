Part Design & Geometric Modeling (Siemens NX)

The goal of this project was to design a 3D ergonomic computer mouse component specifically tailored for 3-axis CNC milling constraints on a Haas VF-2 CNC machine.

The model was created in Siemens NX incorporating revolved side thumb rests, blended top radii (R = 1.5" rear, R = 1.0" front), a 0.2" center slot pocket, corner mounting holes, and smooth organic surface transitions designed to test complex 3D surface milling toolpaths.

CAM Toolpath Programming & Feeds/Speeds

Machining toolpaths were configured in Siemens NX CAM using a Machining Coordinate System (MCS) set on the top left corner of the stock blank with a 0.5" clearance plane above the workpiece.

CNC Machine Setup & Execution (Haas VF-2)

The 6061-T6 aluminum blank was cut on a horizontal band saw and faced on a manual mill before loading into the Haas VF-2 vise with parallels.

  • WCS Edge Finder Zeroing: The X and Y work offsets were zeroed using a 0.2" diameter edge finder, compensating for the 0.1" tip radius.
  • Tool Height Z Offsets: Each tool was loaded into the Haas tool carousel and zeroed against a precision 2" gauge block.
  • Tool Clearance & Collision Prevention: Verified a minimum tool stickout of 1.5" to maintain 0.050" clearance between the tool holder and the workpiece.
  • Surface Finish Result: The 1/4" ball end mill area milling routine produced a smooth, reflective surface finish with no visible scallop lines.
