Hi, I'm Danish Galebotswe

I am a Mechanical Engineering student at Northwestern University with a deep passion for high-performance automotive engineering, aerodynamics, CAD/CAM manufacturing, mechatronics, and machine learning.

As the Aerodynamics Lead for Northwestern Formula Racing SAE, I direct an 8-person engineering team designing and validating comprehensive aerodynamic packages for our Formula Student race car. My technical experience spans over 100+ hours of Ansys Fluent and Siemens Star CCM+ CFD simulations, carbon fiber composite curing, CNC milling on Haas machines, embedded control in C++/Python, and PyTorch deep learning models.

Through engineering internships at ITW MTS Systems Corporation and Asahi Kasei Bioprocess America, I have developed a strong industrial background in mechanical testing, hydraulic systems, P&ID documentation, and rapid prototyping.
