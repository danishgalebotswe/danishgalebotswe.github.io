Executive Summary & EV Powertrain Transition

When Northwestern Formula Racing transitioned from an internal combustion (IC) engine to an electric vehicle (EV) powertrain for NFR23, the differential mounting architecture required a complete redesign.

In NFR22, the 3-part mounting assembly attached directly to the IC engine block. For NFR23, with a compact electric motor, the mounts were redesigned into a 2-part assembly attached directly to the tubular chassis frame. This new configuration supports the 160 mm Drexler differential, withstands intense motor torque, enables rapid chain tensioning via turnbuckle rotation, and acts as compliant rear impact protection per FSAE regulations.

Rule Compliance & Functional Objectives

  • FSAE Rule F.11.2.2 (Rear Impact Protection): Differential mounts extend ≥ 25 mm past upper and lower rear impact tubes, satisfying mandatory structural equivalency requirements.
  • FSAE Rule F.11.2.3 & F.11.2.4 (Accumulator Clearance): Ensures ≥ 25 mm clearance between non-crushable drivetrain items and the high-voltage accumulator container.
  • Kinematic Chain Tensioning: Mounts pivot around fixed top tab frame hinges. Adjusting lower turnbuckles raises/lowers the differential position by > 0.5 inches for rapid chain installation and tension tuning.
  • Press-Fit Bearing Housing Sizing: Engineered press-fit bearing bore tolerances (Left: 89.9715 mm; Right: 79.9735 mm) housing SKF 6011-2Z and SKF 6010-2Z bearings.

Topology Optimization & FEA Stress Results

Because the drive chain is positioned on the left side of the vehicle, the left differential mount experiences severe chain tension loads (9,987 N upward), while the right mount experiences -1,730 N downward load. SolidWorks Topology Optimization was run to strip non-critical material while preserving tab pin holes and bearing housings.

CAM Programming & Haas CNC Manufacturing

Both differential mounts were precision-machined in-house from a single 13" x 9" x 1.5" block of 7075-T6 aluminum using a 3-axis Haas CNC Mill. CAM toolpaths were generated in Fusion 360.

  • Op 1 (Facing): 3-inch face mill to flat-surface the 7075-T6 aluminum stock blank.
  • Op 2 (Adaptive & Contouring): 3/4" flat indexable end mill and 12mm flat end mill for 2D adaptive clearing of bearing pockets and pocket weight-reduction pockets.
  • Drilling & Profiling: 1/8" center drill and 1/4" drill for chassis tab pin holes; 1/4" end mill 2D contouring to cut out outer mount profile.
  • Manual Mill Slotting: Angled 30° and 45° angle blocks used on manual mill to machine turnbuckle attachment slots with a 5/8" end mill.
  • Turnbuckle Machining: Turned from 2 ft low-carbon steel hex bar on lathe, drilled with 0.213" bit, and tapped with RH and LH 1/4-28 threads for dual-direction tensioning.
