Executive Summary & Leadership

As the Aerodynamics Subteam Lead for Northwestern Formula Racing (NFR25), I directed an 8-member engineering subteam responsible for the design, computational fluid dynamics (CFD) analysis, composite manufacturing, structural validation, and on-track testing of the complete car aerodynamic body package.

The primary objective was to deliver a high-downforce, low-drag aerodynamic system (front wing, underbody venturi tunnels, rear wing, and sidepods) tailored for tight autocross cornering while maintaining aerodynamic stability across dynamic vehicle roll, pitch, and yaw motion.

3D Ansys Fluent CFD & Full-Car Aero Mapping

To accurately characterize vehicle performance under dynamic cornering, pitch (braking/acceleration), and yaw (sideslip) conditions, I executed over 100+ hours of full-body 3D Ansys Fluent CFD simulations.

  • Mesh Quality Assurance: Standardized volume meshing protocols with prism layer refinement around complex multi-element airfoils to maintain \(y^+ \approx 1\) for accurate boundary layer shear stress prediction.
  • Pitch & Ride Height Sensitivity: Evaluated front wing ground effect clearance from 25mm to 65mm, quantifying downforce migration under heavy front-axle braking load transfers.
  • Yaw Angle Aero Mapping: Simulated vehicle sideslip angles (\(\beta = 0^\circ, 3^\circ, 6^\circ, 9^\circ\)) to measure crosswind stability and tail wake vortex shedding onto the rear wing elements.

Composite Manufacturing & Operations

To achieve strict weight targets while maintaining structural rigidity against aerodynamic forces exceeding 400 N at 60 mph, we transitioned to precision Routed MDF male/female mold tooling and out-of-autoclave prepreg carbon fiber processing.

  • Mold Fabrication: CAM toolpaths programmed in Siemens NX and CNC-routed out of high-density MDF blocks, followed by sealed epoxy surface finishing.
  • Layup & Vacuum Bagging: Applied 3-layer 3K twill carbon fiber prepreg with epoxy resin system, debulking under 29 inHg vacuum pressure.
  • Oven Cure Protocol: Controlled ramped thermal cure cycles to prevent resin voids, reducing overall manufacturing cycle time by 25%.
  • Sponsorship Negotiation: Successfully negotiated $15,000 in cash and in-kind corporate sponsorships (composite materials, tooling resin, CNC machining time) to fund team manufacturing operations.
