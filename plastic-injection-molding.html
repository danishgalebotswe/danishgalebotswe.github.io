Product Design & Design for Injection Molding (DfIM)

Our team engineered a modular, customizable Among Us character featuring interchangeable accessories: a mustache (attaching to a face hole) and a top hat (attaching to a head pin).

To prevent sink marks, differential shrinkage, and warping, wall thicknesses were maintained at a uniform 0.075 inches for body sections and 0.05 inches for accessories. All vertical faces were assigned 2-degree draft angles, and interior fillets were sized at half the wall thickness to distribute thermal stress evenly.

Interference Fit Stress Calculation:
Pin Diameter (Drilled #30): d = 0.1285 in
Hole Diameter (Press-fit Pin): d1 = 0.1250 in
Interference: I = d - d1 = 0.0035 in
Calculated Design Stress ≈ 60% Yield Stress of Polypropylene (Optimal range for repeatable press-fit).

Aluminum Mold Core/Cavity Tooling & CAM Manufacturing

Four separate aluminum mold plates (Core & Cavity for Front/Mustache and Core & Cavity for Back/Hat) were programmed in CAM and machined on a 3-axis Haas Mini Mill 2.

  • Runner & Gate Balancing: Designed a dual runner system with cold slug wells to equalize plastic flow front arrival times between unequal volume cavities (Hat vs. Body).
  • CAM Machining Toolpaths: Executed 3D Adaptive Milling for bulk roughing (3/4" & 1/2" flat end mills), Cavity Mill semi-finishing (1/4" flat end mill), and Area Mill surface finishing (1/8" ball end mill & 1/8" tapered flat end mill for draft walls).
  • Precision Reaming: Performed secondary reaming operations using a 0.1245" reamer to achieve exact 0.125" dowel pin press-fit alignment.

Injection Molding Process Parameters & Metrology

Initial molding trials produced short shots or flash. By iteratively adjusting barrel temperature, injection pressure, and holding time, optimal parameters were established:

Mold Sub-Assembly Molding Temperature Injection Pressure Cycle Time Final Part Quality
Front Body & Mustache Mold 487°F (252°C) 2,750 psi 7.3 seconds ✅ Full Fill, Zero Flash
Back Body & Top Hat Mold 500°F (260°C) 3,750 psi 4.2 seconds ✅ Full Fill, Zero Flash
